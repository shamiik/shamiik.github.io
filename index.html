I am seeking PhD opportunities in my research interest areas: Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, Hardware Security, Cybersecurity, and TinyML.

I am a Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), Dhaka, Bangladesh, conducting research with the NexGEn Research Group. I also work as a Software Engineer (ML) at Sincos Automation Technologies Ltd., specializing in IoT automation.

With an academic background in electrical and electronic engineering and professional experience in machine learning, I work at the intersection of AI research and practical engineering applications. My research experience includes medical image analysis, deep learning, IoT security, time-series forecasting, and intelligent systems for agriculture and industrial automation.

My research interests span these areas, and I welcome opportunities to pursue doctoral research and collaborate with researchers working on related problems.
