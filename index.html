I'm a Research Assistant in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST) and a Software Engineer at Sincos Automation Technologies Ltd., working across computer vision, deep learning, and biomedical image analysis. I'm currently preparing PhD applications, aiming to work at the intersection of machine learning and healthcare imaging. My research centers on building CNN and dual-backbone architectures for diagnosing complex conditions from medical imagery — brain MRI, ocular fundus photography, and beyond — with an emphasis on explainability and reliability under uncertainty.

Research Focus

  • Explainable biomedical computer vision: dual-backbone, attention-fused architectures for differentiating tumor types from brain MRI (glioma vs. meningioma), and self-attention hybrid networks for multi-class ocular disease classification — both designed around clinician-interpretable outputs, not just raw accuracy.
  • Uncertainty-aware diagnostic AI: incorporating uncertainty estimation into classification pipelines so models communicate confidence, not just predictions, for high-stakes healthcare use.
  • Resource-efficient deep learning: lightweight, self-attention-based architectures built with edge deployment and mobile clinical screening in mind.
  • Applied ML beyond healthcare: time-series forecasting for industrial voltage anomaly detection, graph-based intrusion detection for IIoT networks, and forecasting/prediction work spanning solar irradiance and EV energy consumption — reflecting a broader interest in applying rigorous ML methodology across domains.

Current Work

At AUST, I'm leading the design of a maturity-classification pipeline for Bangladeshi agricultural crops — from data acquisition and annotation protocol to model development — structured for public release to support reproducible agri-AI research. At Sincos, I split my time between deploying ML pipelines for industrial monitoring and building the frontend of an OEE analytics platform.
