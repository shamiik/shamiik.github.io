I’m a Graduate Research Assistant in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST) and Software Engineer at Sincos Automation, working on computer vision, deep learning, and biomedical image analysis. I am actively preparing for PhD applications for Fall 2026. My research develops robust convolutional neural network architectures and vision transformers to detect and classify complex anomalies from medical imagery (MRI, CT, cytology, and fundus photography) with explainable visual attention.

Research focus

  • Biomedical computer vision: detecting and mitigating diagnostic uncertainties in medical imagery with automated tumor segmentation and multi-class cancer grading in histopathology and MRI
  • Explainable artificial intelligence (XAI): clinician-interpretable saliency mapping (Grad-CAM, Score-CAM) and transparent decision reasoning for high-stakes healthcare AI
  • Resource-efficient deep learning: lightweight neural architectures and attention mechanisms optimized for edge healthcare diagnostics and mobile clinical screening
